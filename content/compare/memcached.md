## Reliable at scale: Redis vs Memcached

[Redis](https://redis.io/about/) and [Memcached](https://memcached.org/about) are both in-memory key-value data stores, but we offer more features, flexibility, and speed. Learn how our multi-model database handles caching plus a range of use cases, including [session stores](https://redis.io/solutions/session-store/), [messaging,](https://redis.io/solutions/messaging/) and [real-time operational data storage](https://redis.io/solutions/fast-data-ingest/).

## Why choose Redis over Memcached?

Redis offers superior in-memory data storage with advanced features and better performance for modern applications. Memcached may serve your basic caching needs well, but you probably need more; when it comes to Memcached alternatives, Redis universally comes to mind. Redis’s versatility and reliability make it ideal for session management, real-time data operations and much more.

## Start with Redis today

Migrating from Memcached to Redis is easy. Our [strings](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/data-types/strings/) data type provides similar functionality to Memcached plus higher scalability and availability. Plus we offer [RIOTX](https://redis-field-engineering.github.io/riotx/) to support live migrations.

For a fresh start, and to utilize our advanced functionality, check out the [docs](https://redis.io/docs/latest/develop/get-started/) or enroll in our Redis University course [Introduction to Redis data structures](https://university.redis.com/courses/ru101/).
