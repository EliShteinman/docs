## Real-time decisions that drive real business value

Every company today is sitting on mountains of data – but the real challenge isn’t collecting it. It’s acting on it, instantly.

That’s why Redis and Stacker Group have joined forces to launch a new Next Best Action (NBA) and Next Best Offer (NBO) solution. Together, we help organizations move from slow, batch-based decision-making to real-time intelligence, where every customer interaction, click, or transaction triggers the most relevant, valuable action automatically.

This partnership brings together three powerful components into a new Real-time Decisioning Stack (RDS):

- **Redis**: The real-time data platform built for speed and scale.
- **Redis Data Integration (RDI)**: The bridge that keeps data continuously fresh and connected across systems.
- **Stacker Group’s AI engine, Relight Decision Memory (RDM): T**he brain that learns from every interaction to help your models predict and optimize what happens next. Relight Decision Memory comes from Stacker’s AI Lab, where cutting-edge AI methods are developed.

The result: a unified, intelligent system that continuously learns, adapts, and delivers measurable business outcomes.

---

## From reactive to real-time: Why this matters

Most organizations still make decisions using models that are trained offline and updated occasionally. That’s like navigating using yesterday’s map.

The Redis × Stacker solution changes that. It allows businesses to:

- **React in real time** to customer behavior, risk signals, or operational events.
- **Continuously improve decisions** through built-in feedback loops.
- **Eliminate data delays** that slow down marketing, risk, and CX teams.

With Redis at its core, every decision is powered by live, accurate data. No waiting, no batch updates.

---

## What this looks like in practice

Imagine you’re a digital bank, an e-commerce brand, or a mobility platform.

When a user logs in, adds an item to a cart, or submits a transaction:

1. Redis captures and streams that event instantly.
1. RDI ensures that Redis has the latest customer and contextual data.
1. Stacker’s AI engine, Relight Decision Memory (RDM), gives you the context required to analyze the event and recommends the best next step: maybe a personalized offer, a risk flag, or a proactive notification.
1. Redis executes that action through your app or service, all within milliseconds.

This is the Next Best Action loop in motion—learning, deciding, and acting continuously.

## Smarter experiences, safer interactions

One of the most powerful use cases is something we call Adaptive Routing — a way to balance customer experience with trust and safety.

For example:

- Low-risk customers are fast-tracked with instant approvals.
- Medium-risk transactions trigger extra checks automatically.
- High-risk scenarios route to manual or AI-assisted review.

This means better security without slowing down good customers.

## How Redis Data Integration (RDI) fits in

If Redis is the “engine” of this solution, RDI is the fuel line — keeping everything flowing smoothly.

RDI connects your existing systems (databases, applications, data lakes, and event streams) directly into Redis Enterprise. This means that:

- Customer profiles are always up-to-date.
- AI models get fresh, live features to make better predictions.
- Teams can see and respond to changes the moment they happen.

It removes the traditional barriers between analytics, operations, and decisioning — turning your data ecosystem into a single, synchronized real-time network.
