Today we are pleased to kick off 2014 with the announcement of the immediate availability of Redis Cloud & Memcached Cloud services off two additional AWS data regions:

- **us-west-2** (Oregon)
- **ap-southeast-1** (Singapore)

We’ve added these regions to our services to meet the demand to regions other than AWS’s us-east-1 and eu-west-1, and Azure’s us-east and us-west, where we already have many active users. The details about all of our new and existing plans can be found, as always, at [www.redis.com/pricing](/pricing).

If your application is using compute resources in these data regions and you want to use our services then there’s really nothing to it:

1. If you haven’t already done so, sign up to our service (no credit card needed, no spam sent).
1. Log in to your account.
1. Add a subscription to one of our free or paid new plans.

**You’re done!** Now you can create a cloud instance in seconds and start using it immediately with your application just by changing its connection endpoint.
