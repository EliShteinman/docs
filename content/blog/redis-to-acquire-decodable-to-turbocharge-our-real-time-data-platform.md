*By Rowan Trollope, CEO · Published 4 September 2025 · updated 5 September 2025*

![Redis to acquire decodable](/images/site-mirror/89680cc219482ae8dcbd7db02d177dccdbd2a6ed-1544x1104.webp)

Today, Redis announced that we intend to acquire [Decodable](https://www.decodable.co/), a real-time data platform that lets organizations quickly build, process, and manage streaming pipelines. We’re incredibly excited about this addition to Redis, both for the technology we plan on tightly integrating into our platform, but also the talented people joining us from Decodable.

This acquisition will be a strategic step forward in our mission to be the fastest real-time data platform. As the world pushes deeper into the age of LLMs, one of the biggest challenges developers will face is ensuring the right context goes to the right place at the right time. We’re helping to solve this by strengthening our capabilities as a real-time context engine that enables instant data access for agents.

The data agents need can come from any number of sources—data warehouses, operational databases, unstructured data storage, user inputs, web APIs, and more. Getting that data from where it lives into the context window at the right time, in real time is an incredibly complex task.

[Redis Data Integration (RDI)](https://redis.io/data-integration/) is a key part of our solution to this. It synchronizes data from legacy databases into Redis in near-real time, so your data is always in sync and always fast and ready to use, no matter where it’s stored. Decodable will help us turbocharge RDI, giving it a wider breadth of capabilities and connections so more of your data will be available in Redis. This dramatically simplifies the process of building context pipelines for AI agents and modern applications.

Adding Decodable also reflects our continuing commitment to invest heavily in Redis Cloud. Decodable is a cloud-first platform, so we will begin integrating its capabilities into Redis Cloud immediately. We plan to bring it to RDI on Redis Software soon.

We want Redis to be the center of every data platform, making every part of the stack faster. The more data that flows through Redis, the more powerful and responsive applications and AI systems become. With Decodable, Redis customers will be able to:

- Easily connect diverse data sources to Redis in real time
- Ensure context is always fresh and available for agents and applications
- Build faster, smarter, and more adaptive user experiences

Our vision is that Redis is at the center of every modern data stack, enabling instant access and faster innovation, while powering the next wave of AI experiences. We’re thrilled to welcome the Decodable team and technology into the Redis family to help us get there.
