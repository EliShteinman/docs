*By Mike  Moss , SVP, Worldwide Channels & Alliances · Published 3 September 2025*

![Redis partner program](/images/site-mirror/8218cd81005f626b79b2434951298c2e9e623fd9-772x552.webp)

Redis is excited to announce the upcoming launch of our new Partner Program, set to debut in February 2026. This program is a big step forward in how we work with our global AI, SI and Cloud partners. It is designed to make engaging with Redis sellers consistent, clear, and growth-based at every stage.

Here’s a look at what’s coming:

**One program: **No matter where you do business, you’ll see the same structure, expectations, and benefits. This global consistency makes it easier to integrate Redis into your practice with confidence.

**Role-based learning paths: **We invest in sales and technical certification tracks that make sure your teams are ready and trustworthy. This will help you deliver Redis solutions that make a difference.

**Incentives for growth: **Our structured incentives are built to reward partners who invest in expanding their Redis practice, offering predictability and profitability at scale.

**Clear path to success: **A new transaction and certification framework ensures transparency into program tiers, requirements, and the benefits unlocked as you advance.

This is just the beginning. We’ll share the full details at launch, but the direction is clear: Redis is raising the bar for how we partner with you. Together, we’re building momentum for the next era of innovation and growth. And you don’t have to wait until February to see the momentum. Check out how partners are showing up at [Redis Released](https://redis.io/released/) - our global event series where the ecosystem comes to life.

Stay tuned - we can’t wait to share more in the coming months.
