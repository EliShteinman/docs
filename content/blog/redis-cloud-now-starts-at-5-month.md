![Blog tile image](/images/site-mirror/28424c6c1d732c072a5af518f15d6ade07873016-772x552.webp)

You want to spend your time building, not managing your budget. Today, we’re excited to share that [Redis Cloud](/cloud/) is now more accessible than ever, with the Essentials plan starting at just $5 per month!

It’s the perfect way for you to power a variety of use cases like [caching](/solutions/caching/), [session storage](/solutions/session-management/), and even AI and LLM-powered applications. This fixed monthly pricing model simplifies budgeting for projects, whether you’re just getting started, or scaling up!

### Built for your stack

- **Supported client libraries:** Redis Cloud is more than just the most cost-effective cloud-managed Redis offering. It supports client libraries for the most popular programming languages, ensuring seamless integration with your existing tech stack. Whether you’re working with Python, Java, Node.js, or any other major language, Redis Cloud has got you covered.
- **Seamless integration with cloud providers:** Our strategic partnerships with key cloud providers like AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure mean that integrating Redis Cloud into your environment is smoother than ever. This ensures that you can deploy the best of Redis on the cloud infrastructure of your choice.

### By developers, for developers

Redis Cloud isn’t just a cost-saving solution. It’s the culmination of expertise from the developers who created [Redis Open Source](https://redis.io/) and [Redis Enterprise](/enterprise/). This expertise translates into unique functionalities like [JSON](https://redis.io/docs/data-types/json/), [Search](/search/), and exclusive [Vector database](/solutions/vector-search/) capabilities. With Redis Cloud, you’re not just choosing a service; you’re opting for a solution packed with innovative features that you won’t find anywhere else.

### Beyond the Essentials

For those who need even more, our Pro plan offers everything in the Essentials and much more. If your requirements include 99.999% availability and round-the-clock support, the Pro plan is your go-to choice. It’s designed to cater to the most demanding applications, providing peace of mind and unparalleled support.

### Getting started

To get started, visit the[ pricing page here](/cloud/pricing/) and select the Essentials plan to sign up for a new Redis Cloud account. If you’ve already got a Cloud account, use the[ login page](https://app.redislabs.com/) and create a new subscription. There, you’ll find the new plans and can get started with just a few clicks!

### Final thoughts

As a developer, you understand the importance of reliable, efficient, and cost-effective tools in your stack. With Redis Cloud, you’re getting more than just another cloud service – we’re committed to providing you with an increasingly powerful, versatile, and affordable solution for your database needs. With Redis Cloud, you’re not just saving money; you’re investing in a platform that grows and evolves with your projects.

Redis Cloud is here to change the game. **What will you build?**
