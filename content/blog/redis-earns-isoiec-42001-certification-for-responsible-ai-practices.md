Every day, Redis is dedicated to delivering effective and reliable solutions through our products and services. We also know that trust must be earned, not simply asserted.

That’s why we’re excited to announce that Redis is among the first organizations globally to achieve ISO/IEC 42001 certification. This significant accomplishment reflects our unwavering commitment to providing customers with transparency, accountability, and assurance as they entrust us with their most critical data.

## How does Redis’ ISO/IEC 42001 certification benefit Redis customers?

ISO/IEC 42001 is the international standard for Artificial Intelligence Management Systems (AIMS). This internationally recognized framework outlines the requirements for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving policies and controls to ensure the responsible and effective management of AI technologies. Obtaining this certification affirms our dedication to managing the ethical, societal and legal concerns associated with AI.

This certificate validates that Redis provides a secure and compliant foundation for the responsible development and deployment of AI-powered applications. To earn this distinction, we underwent a rigorous, independent audit by an accredited third-party body, during which our AI governance, transparency, and risk management practices were examined in depth and measured against the requirements of ISO/IEC 42001.

We view this certification as more than a milestone; it is a testament to our dedication to upholding the highest standards in artificial intelligence management for all Redis customers.

All documentation related to Redis’ ISO/IEC 42001 certification—as well as additional materials addressing security, trust, and compliance—can be accessed through the Redis Customer Trust Center at [trust.redis.io](https://trust.redis.io).
