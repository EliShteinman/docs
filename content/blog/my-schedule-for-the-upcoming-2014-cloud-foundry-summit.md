*By Redis   · Published 28 May 2014 · updated 27 March 2025*

![Blog tile image](/images/site-mirror/0073e867b4e64f59de9047fdcde04295ba647e5c-772x550.webp)

In less than two weeks, Pivotal is holding its annual [Cloud Foundry summit](https://cfsummit.com/) in San Francisco and our team is gearing up for the event. Cloud Foundry has been and will continue to be a pivotal element in our company’s vision, and we’re already using it extensively with [Pivotal Web Services](/blog/now-generally-available-on-pivotal-web-services).

I’m looking forward to travelling there to participate in the event, and have prepared a list of sessions I hope to attend (and that I’d recommend for anyone else who’s going):

#### Day 1

- 6pm-7:30pm [Keynotes](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/53628ed5ecdcc4061200005a) – It’s always great to hear the big announcements first hand.

#### Day 2

- 12pm-12:30pm [IBM BlueMix lightning talk](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/5371088eecdcc40b9e000012) – It will be interesting to compare with our experience on that platform.
- 2:30pm-3pm [Autoscaling Distributed Systems with BOSH](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/53628196ecdcc4f78d000055) – This is exactly what we’re doing with Redis Cloud.
- 3pm-3:30pm I wish I could be in two place simultaneously: [CF comparison](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/5362893cecdcc4fb6a000007) or [OSv](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/537bc5ceecdcc42c49000013)
- 3:30pm-4:pm [Add-on services](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/53628059ecdcc4c44c00001c) – This should be very relevant to our offers on Pivotal Web Services, the IBM Marketplace and BlueMix.
- 4pm-4:30pm *Here I’ll be relaxing and preparing for…*
- 4:30pm-5pm [**How to Build a High Performance Application using Cloud Foundry and Redis**](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/536a5ee3ecdcc44f78000004) (that’s my session and I promise it’ll be a great opportunity to learn about Redis and how to use it on Cloud Foundry for a wide range of use cases)

#### Day 3

- 1:45pm-2:15pm [Cloud Foundry roadmap](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/537e3d07ecdcc4550b0000a9)
- 2:30pm-3pm [Multi data center deployments](https://2014.event.cfsummit.com/schedule/53628e6aecdcc40612000057) – It would be great to catch this one, as it’s something we’re also working on.

When not in the sessions, I’ll be in meetings or at our booth. I don’t want to spoil the surprise, but we’ve also prepared a new demo that is I feel is really impressive – I recommend that you come by the booth to check it out. If you want to book a meeting with me in advance, feel free to email me directly at [yiftach@redis.com](mailto:yiftach@redis.com?subject=Cloud%20Foundry%20Summit%202014).
