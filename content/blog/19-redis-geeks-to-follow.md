*By Redis   · Published 10 February 2020 · updated 27 March 2025*

![Blog tile image](/images/blog/635e149720b6124b8ebe851aba9fd52fb1ce4dc1-772x550.webp)

Keeping up with the latest Redis news and information isn’t always easy. Redis community events like [RedisConf](/redisconf/), [RedisDays](/redisdays/), and various meet-ups are great, immersive experiences, but they come around only so often and not everyone can attend an in-person event.

Thanks to the magic of the internet and social media, though, you can stay on top of all things Redis right from your computer or phone. Follow the folks on this list of some of our favorite “Online Redis Geeks” and you’ll always be in the know. (The list starts with Redis folks, but also includes Redis experts from a wide variety of backgrounds and regions.)

## Salvatore Sanfilippo

![Salvatore Sanfillipo](/images/blog/2b60de778ff0089d066ae514e1e8ced9e582b1ab-175x175.webp)

You can’t talk about Redis without mentioning its creator, Salvatore Sanfilippo. Salvatore frequently tweets and blogs about his latest projects, including big events like the release candidate of Redis 6!

Twitter: [@antirez](https://twitter.com/antirez)

Blog: [http://antirez.com/latest/0](http://antirez.com/latest/0)

GitHub: [/redis](https://github.com/redis/redis)

## Yossi Gottlieb

![Yossi Gottlieb](/images/blog/5f631e81b22d1c8ed82634af71c95abafcd20501-500x500.webp)

Yossi Gottlieb, Redis’s chief architect, is an experienced tech leader with diverse software engineering experience in various fields. He’s far more active on Github than on Twitter.

GitHub: [@yossigo](https://github.com/yossigo)

## Oran Agra

![Oran Agra](/images/blog/93bcad5648204b6c32631889d813fdb753b0bcaf-125x125.webp)

Oran Agra is a senior software architect at Redis. His background is in real-time and embedded systems, and he puts his expertise on display on the Redis core, Redis on Flash, and CRDTs-based Active-Active replication. He’s definitely worth a follow on Github.

GitHub: [@oranagra](https://github.com/oranagra)

## Guy Royse

![Guy Royse](/images/blog/4a7b8c915ea6e89a04c968361cbe791421f8eade-1024x1024.webp)

With his striking gray beard, it’s hard to miss the newest Redis developer advocate. Guy Royse has more than 25 years of developer experience, but he hardly touched Redis until he joined Redis. Read about his experience with Redis as a Noob, and catch his thoughts on everything from coding to pop culture on Twitter.

Twitter: [@guyroyse](https://twitter.com/guyroyse)

GitHub: [@guyroyse](https://github.com/guyroyse)

## Itamar Haber

![Itamar Haber](/images/blog/c80f54bc4cb4ee002894abf01fb6b6151c9fdada-460x460.webp)

As Redis’ first Technology Evangelist, Itamar Haber knows Redis inside and out. Follow him for consistent Redis technology commentary, insights, tutorials, and conversation addressing everything from [adding a local cache with Redis 6](https://twitter.com/itamarhaber/status/1215317542765461504?s=20) to [Redis milestones and commands timelines](https://twitter.com/itamarhaber/status/1204450852192366593?s=20).

Twitter: [@itamarhaber](https://twitter.com/itamarhaber)

GitHub: [@itamarhaber](https://github.com/itamarhaber)

## Marc Gravell

## Nick Craver

![Nick Craver](/images/blog/f19dfb8059c3a82297f83b136e21efe4a4cb888f-400x400.webp)

Nick is an Architect Lead for Stack Exchange and contributor to [StackExchange.Redis](https://github.com/StackExchange/StackExchange.Redis). He frequently discusses his work at Stack Overflow—often touching on Redis. For example, in a [2019 blog post](https://nickcraver.com/blog/2019/08/06/stack-overflow-how-we-do-app-caching/) about Stack Overflow’s caching system, he asks: “Why Redis and not <something else>? Well, because it works. And it works well. It seemed like a good idea when we needed a shared cache. It’s been incredibly rock solid. We don’t wait on it—it’s incredibly fast.”

Twitter: [@nick_craver](https://twitter.com/Nick_Craver)

Website: [https://nickcraver.com/](https://nickcraver.com/)

GitHub: [@nickcraver](https://github.com/NickCraver)

## Carlos Justiniano

He also wrote the [Hydra framework](https://www.hydramicroservice.com/), a Node.js package that leverages Redis to facilitate building distributed applications, including microservices architectures. A two-time RedisConf speaker, he writes frequently on Medium.
