###### Challenge

Purplle needed to deliver personalized, localized beauty experiences to a growing user base of over 10 million monthly active users. A key focus was expanding access to customers in non-metro and regional markets—many of whom were previously underserved by high-quality beauty offerings.

To support this growth, Purplle required real-time infrastructure that could scale with unpredictable spikes in traffic, manage session states reliably across devices, and handle complex workloads, like live pricing updates and inventory sync, across 60K+ SKUs and 10+ warehouses. Their legacy systems struggled with autoscaling, low-latency personalization, and managing high-concurrency workloads at scale.

> Redis is reliable, resilient, and built for scale. It powers our most critical systems—from pricing to personalization—while letting us focus on delivering innovation to our customers.

###### Solution

Purplle adopted Redis to support mission-critical workloads, including real-time pricing, session management, semantic search, and personalization. Redis powers their pricing engine—capable of handling 60K–300K price updates per second—and supports real-time inventory syncing across multi-tenant environments. Purplle's fast, reliable architecture helps it deliver smooth customer experiences even at high volume. The engineering team uses Redis to create highly personalized recommendations and 1:1 targeting in under 100ms.

### Key benefits & results

- **Hyper-scalability**
Purplle powers 90B+ monthly data points and 750M+ CRM messages with elastic scaling.
- **Personalization at scale**
The team enables real-time, per-user recommendations and search.
- **Operational resilience**
The company now operates on a reliable infrastructure with no major incidents in 2.5 years.
- **Speed and simplicity**
Lightning-fast pricing updates and session handling at scale are the new norm for the business.

### Technology in Action

- **Redis cloud:** Supports pricing engine, session state, CRM delivery, and inventory sync
- **Clustered Redis architecture:** Enables high availability and horizontal scaling across workloads
- **Semantic search:** Local-language discovery powered by vector-based GenAI capabilities
- **Omnichannel integration:** Delivers seamless offline and online customer experiences

###### Next Steps

Purplle is investing in AI-driven content generation using their internal GenAI platform. Redis will remain central to powering fast, personalized video experiences at scale—helping customers discover the right beauty products through real-time, data-driven recommendations.
