## Redis for feature stores

## How it works

### Get ultra-low-latency predictions

Real-time AI apps need immediate responses, and we deliver sub-millisecond predictions. That means we can support the most time-sensitive operations, including fraud detection, dynamic pricing, and recommendation systems where fast decisions and responses are key.

### Integrate with existing tech stacks

We work seamlessly with top offline stores, like Tecton and Feast, to integrate into existing ML workflows. This streamlines data management by accessing, storing, and retrieving ML features directly and ensures smooth transitions between storage layers so apps work better and get to production faster.

### Reduce downtime

Our five-nines availability and single-digit-second failover are crucial for mission-critical AI apps, delivering data consistency and uninterrupted operations, minimizing downtime, protecting data integrity, and maintaining smooth workflow continuity.
